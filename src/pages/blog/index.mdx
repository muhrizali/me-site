When I got to know about a note-taking application called Obsidian a few years back, I hadn't given it much thought, assuming its not anything special. But always interested in trying out new applications, I learnt a little about it from YouTube and from its own website. The words "local", "private" and "linking your thinking" convinced me to try it out. Never had the slightest impression that it would change my life.

Obsidian has made me fall in love with writing. It has compelled me to open the application whenever I turn my computer on without even thinking. I started with it by taking notes for the things I was learning at the time. When I learnt about Zettlekasten, I also started to journal about my day and week, and things that I want to capture from them.

Writing has reinforced the type of person I am and the type of person I can be. So most of the things I write, I write for myself. It has helped me countless times to come back to my own words to get some revelation about situations I didn't know I needed help with. I am sure some of these writings will help you too.

Here you will find the blog posts about stuff that interests me. That includes topics like religion, literature, philosophy, clarity and discipline.
