## me?

I am Muhriz Ali and it is a little hard to write about yourself. Especially when you have not made time to think about who you are for a long time. I am many things depending on who's asking. 

For people younger than me, I am an IT practitioner and a software developer. For people older than me, I am a life-long student of pragmatic software engineering, Linux operating systems and IT. For people who taught me, I am a person with unimaginable and unending gratitude.

For people who raised me, I am a sight of calmness, serenity and a fortunate answer to their prayers. For people who I considered friends, I am a memory. For some girl, I am a somewhat confused and complicated phase of her life. For my laptop and Kindle, I am that annoying friend who wakes them up at 3 AM. For the rest of the people and for myself, ideally I am just a collective of all things above. But my favorite is the one, where I am a no one.

## this site?

I wanted to create a website for myself (and of myself) for a very long time. When I started working on this site last year, I told a friend that I was working on something in anticipation. So much time has been passed that even she forgot about that after asking for it a couple of times.

There were a lot of things going on at the time when I was developing this site. Sometimes the greed of college credits will sway me away from it, other times I would be learning things of my own interests. There was also the question "will it be worth it in the end?" pounding inside my head.

Fortunately now, only one questions remain. That question is "Is the website up to the point where I want it to be?". The answer to this question has been "No" for sometime and I hope it stays that way.

## why though?

I have created this website to write about things that interests me. These include things happening inside my life, things which are happening around me, subtle things that creeps in between them, and the lessons I get to carry from all of them. I love to write about philosophy, literature, religion, focus, and all things meta.

Through the years of writing about people and my thoughts on Obsidian (incredible application by the way) I realized that none of the activities in a person's life reinforces who they are as much as the writing. When you give words to your abstract ideas and unorganized thoughts, you don't realize how much firm the understanding you have for something.

This was enough for me for getting started to create something that would reinforce this very concept. Wish I could say this is my own fun "Meditations" like Marcus Aurelius but that would be like comparing... actually I don't even have analogy for that. Sorry Marcus.

## but how?

How!? With tears, sweat, blood and some sleepless nights of course. I am not kidding, one day I was thinking about improving the performance of the site at 3 AM on my bed. The ideas came and while trying enact them out, something literally made me get up and made me walk to my laptop. And started working on it.

The primary and dominant framework in creating this website is the awesome, minimal and highly configurable [Astro](https://astro.build/) project. I have also made use of two excellent projects, [AlpineJS](https://alpinejs.dev/start-here) and [HTMX](https://four.htmx.org/) for some essential functions.

I would also like to mention here that _NO generative AI, agentic AI or any kind of AI_ has been used in creating any part (not even a letter) of this site. The first reason being that I do not like being dependent on anyone other than my mind (however imperfect) in the case of skill. Second reason, AI has always been an amazing catalyst in messing up my work and greatly pissing me off.

## reach me?

When I was in 8th grade I was watching a horror movie titled [IT (2017)](https://www.google.com/search?&q=it+chapter+1&oq=it+ch&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8) with my little brother and aunt. It was then, when I was mesmerized by an angel that appeared on screen and got to know her name was [Sophia Lillis](https://www.google.com/search?q=Sophia+Lillis). I wanted to look at more of her. That was the time when I created my Instagram account for the first time. I deleted it after a few days when I finally looked at all of her pictures.
