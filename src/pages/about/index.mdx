It is a little hard to write about yourself when you have not made time to think about that for a long time. I am many things depending on who's asking. 

For people younger than me, I am an IT practitioner and a software developer. For people older than me, I am a life-long student of pragmatic software engineering, Linux operating systems and IT. For people who taught me, I am a person with unimaginable and unending gratitude.

For people who raised me, I am a sight of calmness, serenity and a fortunate answer to their prayers. For people who I considered friends, I was once a memory. For some girl, I was a somewhat confused and complicated phase of her life. For my laptop and Kindle, I am that annoying friend who wakes them up at 3 AM. For the rest of the people and for myself, I am just a collective of all things above or no one at all.
