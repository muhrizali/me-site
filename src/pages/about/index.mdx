# {frontmatter.title}

It is astonishing how much you don't know about yourself when you are about to actually sit down and write about yourself. The same is happening to me right now and I am finding it difficult to come up with words that could define me. 

Do you define yourself in terms of your family? 
Do you define yourself from your race, religion or caste? 
Do you define yourself with the good choices you have made in the past or the bad ones? 
Do you define yourself relative to the people who you have been with at some point in life or the ones you are currently with? 
Do you define yourself with how much you hate or love them? 
Do you define yourself from the education degree you are studying for or job role in the organization you are working with?

I strangely know the answers to all these questions but at the same time don't know the reasoning behind even one. This might be because the answers to all these will always be different the next time I will be on this page. But for the sake of the effort of working on this site for some time, I will try to be more descriptive, as best as I can.

</Writing>
