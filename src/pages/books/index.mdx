My father has always said that whatever you are doing for leisure, watching TV or reading a book, you should have at least one thing to learn from it at the end. At the time I could not figure out what the hell am I supposed to learn from the movie, Ghost Rider (2007). 

But since then I have started observing things a little more closely in my reading. The pace has been slowed down and now I take my time to observe the things the medium really wants me to see. It has allowed me to start learning and seeing the things my father was talking about. Even in the movie Ghost Rider, where the lesson was: _don't sell your soul to the damn devil_.

Whenever I am reading some book, it always feels like I am expanding my mind a little. It does not matter whether the book is good or bad, it always has left me with something. Sometimes a new perspective on a mundane ritual, a new resolution to a problem I didn't know I had or a new understanding to sensitivity towards certain subjects.

**Favorite Books:** A Thousand Splendid Suns / Atomic Habits / The Subtle Art Of Not Giving A F\*ck / The Way Of The Superior Man / Stoner / The Stranger
