<Status date="2026-10-12">
    There has been anticipation of another huge protest in New Delhi. But this time Delhi Police has been prepared along with countless other forces like border patrol units, CRPF, army personnel and many many more. Around 40,000 personnel has been deployed at Jantar Mantar, the designated protest site. 
    
    If you are someone who is not following this and you see them fortifying the site, you will most probably assume that they are preparing for some huge terrorist attack or a war. But you will be mistaken. These preparations are for people in their 20s and 30s, protesting against the illegal deletions of 13 crore voters and suspicious addition of unknown voters in Election Commission Of India (ECI) accounts data.

    This has become a huge issue. But I think the main origin points are just two; the two videos by Rahul Gandhi ([this one](https://youtu.be/NY3m1JEJac0) and [this one](https://youtu.be/gaFoWrF6FxA)) and an investigation by journalist Ritika Chopra. 
    
    One year back, the opposition leader Rahul Gandhi posted two videos on his YouTube channel. The video indicated that there are huge anomalies in the ECI data when he began to examine them. In any another functioning democratic country, these videos would have eradicated the sitting government after thorough investigation.
    
    Not in India though. The institutions here didn't even feel the need to acknowledge there has been something wrong, let alone investigating them. And we people would wake up one year later, when the journalist [Ritika Chopra from The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/express-exclusive/election-commission-sir-14-objections-gyanesh-kumar-sukhbir-singh-sandhu-vivek-joshi-10889737/) wrote an investigative report on the subject.

</Status>
